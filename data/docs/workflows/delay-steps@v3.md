# Delay steps

## Overview

A Delay step pauses a workflow between two actions, such as waiting before
sending a follow-up email.

## Setup

1. Open the workflow builder.
2. Insert a **Delay** step between the two email actions.
3. Choose a duration (minutes, hours, or days).
4. Save and publish the workflow.

## Limits

The maximum delay is **90 days**.
