# Email sending limits

## Daily caps

Sending limits depend on plan and domain verification status. Unverified
domains are capped at a lower daily volume until DNS records pass.

## Soft vs hard limits

Soft limits trigger warnings in the UI. Hard limits pause further sends until
the next reset window.
